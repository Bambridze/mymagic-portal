🪄 Welcome to Your World of Magic!

Introducing an app that turns your smartphone into a magic wand that responds to your voice commands. Just say a spell, and your wand triggers a short video and sound linked to that enchantment. And of course, there are spells to turn your flashlight on and off!

⚡ Quick Start (Skip the Manual)

Want to try your magic in action right away? Here is a quick summary to get you started (you can return to the full manual at any time):

  • Unlock the Entrance: Say the password correctly, and you will enter the app's main area—the Hall.
  • Check Your Spells: Go to Settings (the gear icon in the top right corner) to see which spells are already at your disposal and learn how to pronounce them.
  • Start Casting: Use the toggle on the top left to switch into Casting Mode and start speaking your enchantments!

🎨 Media Customization & Logic

The main feature of this app is complete customization. How your magic looks and sounds is limited only by your imagination! You can use AI generators or any other tools to create your own pictures, videos, and audio.

📂 Managing Your Files:
  • Ready-made Media: You can preview and purchase premium videos via our Telegram channel. Content downloaded from here goes directly into the correct app folder.
  • Free Content: Use the provided link to Pixabay for free assets.
  • Manual File Moving: Files downloaded from websites will end up in your device's standard "Download" folder. You must manually move all .mp4 and .mp3 files to the "MyMagicMedia" folder for the app to recognize them.
🎬 Video & Audio Behavior:
  • Login Scene: The login video (5–10 seconds) is split into two parts. Video One loops continuously at the wall. Once you say the password, Video Two plays, showing the wall crumbling. You can use videos with built-in audio, mute them, or link separate audio files in the settings.
  • The Hall: Media here loops continuously. To prevent lagging, if you set video and audio separately, they must be of the exactly same duration.
  • Spells: Keep files short to guarantee correct playback. Short clips of 3-5 seconds are quite enough.

🧠 Vosk Neural Network & Voice Recognition

The heart of our app is the Vosk neural network—it handles the offline recognition of your spells.

🌐 Language Models & Offline Mode

  • 100% Offline: You do not need an internet connection to use the app. Internet is required only for the initial download of the interface translation or a new language model.
  • Dictionary Constraint: For your spells, you can only use words that actually exist in the dictionary of your chosen language.
  • Switching Languages: Tap the current language icon to open the menu. You can switch between downloaded models (like English and back) at any time. Note: The English language model currently works best!

⚠️ Spell Setup & The Backdoor

  • Grammar Matters: To change spells, type them in the corresponding lines in the settings with correct grammar.
  • Language Match: If you write a spell or password in a language different from the active Vosk model, they will not work.
  • 🚪 The Backdoor: If you change the language and forget to update your password (or just completely forget it), tap the cracked door icon on the login screen to bypass it.

🛠️ Debug Mode

In the settings, you will find a "Debug Mode" switch. This tool was used during development and can be helpful for you:

  • It shows you exactly what Vosk hears in real-time.
  • It displays colored indicator circles to show when a spell triggers.
  • If you don't need this visual data, simply toggle it off.

💎 Premium & Customization Store

By default, the application allows you to change the login password, interface language, and screen scaling for each video. However, full customization is a premium feature:

  • Premium Access: Completely and permanently unlocks all available customization slots in the application.
  • ⚡ Energy Packs: Can be used to unlock specific customization options manually (20 points per item) or to download new videos from our in-app multimedia store (10 points per video).
  • Content Updates: Videos in the store will be continuously added and updated over time!

🔮 Cloud Synchronization & Progress Recovery

The application now features an automatic backup system. It securely stores information about your Premium access, unlocked customization slots, and purchased energy balance in the cloud storage.

🚀 How it works upon first launch:

  • Automatic Start: As soon as you take your first step toward customization (purchase Premium, an energy pack, or watch your first advertisement), the application instantly generates your unique digital ID and sends the first transaction to the cloud.
  • Email Request: Immediately after, the application will prompt you to link your Email. This is necessary solely to ensure you do not lose your progress in the event of a device malfunction or phone upgrade.
  • Receiving Your ID: If you agree, you will receive a verification code. After successfully entering it, an official email will be sent containing your personal Application ID. Make sure to save this email!

⚠️ Important Notice:

  • If you declined the email link: Your progress will still be safely recorded in the cloud under your current device ID. However, if you delete the application or reset your device, recovering this data on a new device will be impossible, as you will not have the email containing your Application ID.
  • Do not rush: There is no need to try and link your email manually in a blank application before your first ad view or purchase. The system has not yet generated your ID, so sending an empty email to the cloud is pointless. Please wait for the automatic pop-up window to appear.

🔄 How to restore progress on a new device:

If you reinstalled the application or switched to a new phone, restore your hard-earned resources in just a couple of steps:

  1. Open Settings -> go to the "Cloud Synchronization" section, and press "Restore Progress".
  2. Enter your linked email and the Application ID found in the very first email you received during registration. Your entire balance (including Premium and Energy) will instantly return to your screen!

💡 Additional Tips & Device Requirements

🗣️ Smart Spell Casting:

  • Avoid False Triggers: Default spells consist of a couple of words or a single long word (like the flashlight spell). We highly recommend using the same structure for your custom spells to prevent accidental activations.
  • Audio Overlays: If you use your own video with custom overlaid audio, you must turn off the default sound using the buttons right next to it for your audio to play properly.
  • Login Screen: Please note that there is no sound on the login lock screen by default—only video playback.

📱 Hardware & OS Compatibility:

  • Flashlight Modes: The app features 2 operating modes for the flashlight. This requires Android 13+ and may be completely unavailable on budget or older devices.
  • Performance: The Vosk neural network runs locally. If your device has weak or outdated hardware, it might not run smoothly.
